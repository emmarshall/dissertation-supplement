November 18, 2026
Emma Marshall, J.D., M.A.
University of Nebraska-Lincoln

This section reports the checks on the narrative coding, the reliability of the holistic codes, the distributions of the ratings, the pairwise comparisons of the crime categories, the scenario-feature prediction checks, and the exploratory analyses. Chapter 5 reports the main descriptive statistics, reliabilities, and results for each research question.

Validation and Reliability of the Narrative Coding

The narrative codes were assigned in three phases. The Phase 2 application flagged 1,718 of 7,464 code cells in the 311 reporting narratives; Phase 1 flagged 0 of 13,373. Table P1.4 shows the cutoff for each phase, and Table P1.5 shows the inter-rater reliability of the holistic Phase 3 codes. The keyness analyses in Table P1.6 and Table P1.7 serve as a convergent check on the coding. They show the words that most set each crime category apart from the others.

Note. The cutoff per phase was to maximize F1 subject to a recall floor of .80, calibrated on a hand-coded 100- to 200-narrative sub-sample; where no cutoff met the floor, the cutoff with the highest recall was used. Codes at or above the cutoff are accepted automatically; codes below are designated for Phase 3 review.

Note. Each estimate appears with its 95% confidence interval and the number of double-coded narratives contributing to it. I used Cohen’s kappa for the binary codes and the intraclass correlation for ordinal and dimensional codes. Five codes had ordered values, so I treated them as ordinal. For these codes, I report both the registered unweighted kappa and the ICC, excluding the not-specified value from each estimate. The preregistered target is kappa >= .75 (binary) or ICC >= .80 (ordinal/dimensional). Media framing was invariant across the subsample, so no coefficient is defined. † Too few narratives specified the construct for both coders to score it; these estimates are reported for completeness and are not reliability evidence in either direction.

Note. Chi-square is the standard keyness chi-square statistic (positive: term is distinctively more common in the target category than in the reference corpus). n (target) and n (reference) are the raw token counts in the target and reference corpora. Added analysis, not registered: a term enters only if it appears in at least two of the 311 narratives.

Scenario-Feature Prediction Checks (H5)

How accurately did each feature predict the crime judgments of participants who had not contributed to its fitted model? I report the prediction comparison as a post-hoc analysis. The likelihood-ratio statistics in the main analyses used different degrees of freedom and did not establish uniquely strongest or weakest features.

I compared separate logistic models, is_crime ~ feature + (1 | participant_id), for the five reported feature codings and the original 13-level method variable. The latter checked the recoding of method as abortion-pill use; gestational age remained untested. I used a binary outcome: definite Yes = 1; No or Maybe = 0. In five-fold cross-validation, I kept each participant’s 20 responses together, fitting each model to 64 or 65 of the 81 participants and predicting responses for the omitted group. The predictions averaged over the fitted normal distribution of participant intercepts. For a training category containing only zeros or only ones, I used a probability of 0 or 1, respectively, without a finite category coefficient.

Mental state and the original method variable had lower prediction errors than pregnancy outcome, care-seeking, abortion-pill use, and medical intervention (Table P1.10; Table P1.11). The interval comparing mental state with original method included zero. Medical intervention ranked last among the five reported codings; its intervals against pregnancy outcome, care-seeking, and abortion-pill use nevertheless included zero.

Sensitivity Checks

Sought care coincided with medical intervention, and delayed care with negligence. These models do not identify independent or causal effects. Prediction on new scenarios was not tested.
