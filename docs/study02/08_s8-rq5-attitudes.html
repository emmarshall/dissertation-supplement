Probe Comparison Delta Pr(award) Delta positive mean Delta expected award 95% CI
Note. I examined whether the SMA − NCM award difference varied with abortion attitudes using a logistic model for any award and a Gamma model with a log link for positive amounts. In this primary adjusted two-part model, each part has six condition intercepts and six abortion-attitude slopes. Within each part, the plaintiff-blame, officer-blame, and other three attitude slopes are common across the six conditions. The Gamma model has one dispersion parameter. For each attitude probe, I calculated the SMA − NCM differences in award probability, positive award amount, and expected award. Expected award is the product of probability and positive amount, including zero awards. Predictions cover the included SMA and NCM participants (n = 213), each in their own evidence condition with equal weight, with attitudes set to the probe. Probe values are the participants’ abortion-attitude mean (3.31) minus and plus one sample SD (1.77). Both lie inside the observed range common to all four SMA and NCM conditions, 1.00 to 6.86. As specified in the registered analysis supplement, the last row subtracts the lower-score difference from the higher-score difference. The 95% interval covers only the expected-award difference. Each participant percentile bootstrap attempt refits both model parts and recalculates its attitude mean and SD. Of 10000 participant resamples drawn within conditions, 9959 were usable per quantity; failed attempts were not replaced. Award-probability and positive-award differences are descriptive components of the same predictions, without intervals. E5b has no registered direction or support gate. Decision for E5b: estimates reported; no registered decision rule.
