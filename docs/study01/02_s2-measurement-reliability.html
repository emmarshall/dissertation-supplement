Notes shared by tables of this section.

  • Tables 1-S2.11, 1-S2.15, 1-S2.17 and 1-S2.20 to 1-S2.22: Added analysis, not registered: evidence relevance is measured by two latent factors from eight of the ten relevance items (items 3 and 10 dropped), and each factor is a separate mediator.
  • Tables 1-S2.11, 1-S2.15, 1-S2.17 and 1-S2.20 to 1-S2.22: For a relevance pathway the estimate is the sum across factors, the quantity the registration names and the one the registered decision rule reads (“the indirect association through each factor and the sum across factors”); the per-factor components carry no decision rule.
  • Tables 1-S2.11, 1-S2.20 and 1-S2.22: Intervals: participant bootstrap percentiles (type 6), 10,000 attempts, 10,000 usable.
  • Tables 1-S2.11, 1-S2.15, 1-S2.17 and 1-S2.20 to 1-S2.22: — = no factor component (not a relevance pathway).
  • Tables 1-S2.11 and 1-S2.20: Primary set: grouped, as in the registered run.
  • Tables 1-S2.11, 1-S2.12, 1-S2.15, 1-S2.16 and 1-S2.20 to 1-S2.22: Expected evidence = evidence consistent with a self-managed abortion; unexpected evidence = financial-crime evidence; each is contrasted with no information.
  • Tables 1-S2.11, 1-S2.12, 1-S2.16 to 1-S2.18 and 1-S2.20 to 1-S2.22: n per condition, primary sample: Illegal abortion: No information 68, Expected evidence 63, Unexpected evidence 63; Improper disposal: No information 71, Expected evidence 66, Unexpected evidence 67 (all 398 in the analysis).
  • Tables 1-S2.12 and 1-S2.18: Paths are in original units, with each relevance factor on its marker item’s 1–9 scale. Robust standard errors supply normal Wald 95% intervals. The a paths vary by legal context; the b paths are shared.
  • Tables 1-S2.13 and 1-S2.19: These models use eight observed relevance items instead of their mean. The fit indices and degrees of freedom are not directly comparable with those of the registered model. Tables 1-S2.13, 1-S2.19, and 1-S2.23 report lavaan’s robust RMSEA with 90% CIs.
  • Tables 1-S2.17 and 1-S2.18: Expected evidence = evidence consistent with a self-managed abortion; unexpected evidence = financial-crime evidence.
