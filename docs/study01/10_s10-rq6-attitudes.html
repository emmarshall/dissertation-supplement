Predicted judgments across abortion attitudes after no information, expected evidence and unexpected evidence

Note. Predictions of the registered analyses of covariance, in which the interaction slopes are the same in both legal contexts. They were computed with marginaleffects from the fitted models and their HC3 coefficient covariance. The legal contexts are weighted equally. Support for punishment, hostile sexism, and benevolent sexism are held at the means of the participants in each model. Bands are pointwise 95% delta-method intervals of each predicted level. Figure 1-S10.2

Fitted sexism, attitude and suspect-punishment chains (E17a–E17d)

Note. The figure shows the fitted single-group path model in the primary sample. E17a–E17d run from hostile and benevolent sexism through support for punishment and abortion attitudes to suspect punishment. E17a and E17b are the total indirect effects of each sexism score (i.e., the sum of its two routes). E17c and E17d are the differences between the two routes of each score. Each path shows its unstandardized estimate and robust (Huber–White) Wald 95% interval in the original units. Solid paths have intervals that exclude zero, and dashed paths have intervals that include zero. The figure omits the outcome-information and legal-context covariates of suspect punishment, the covariance of the two sexism scores, and the covariance of the mediator disturbances. The path table reports them. The indirect effects are products of the paths and appear in the indirect-effect table.
