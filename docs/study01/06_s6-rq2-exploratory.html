Fitted decision-quality chains (E6a–E6f)

Note. E6a–E6b are the direct paths to decision quality, E6c–E6d run through relevance and likelihood, and E6e–E6f run through severity and foreseeability. I fitted the registered H2a–H2b and E6 model in the primary sample with ten-item relevance means. Abortion and Disposal denote illegal abortion and improper disposal. Path labels give unstandardized estimates in the original units and robust (Huber–White) Wald 95% intervals. I estimated outcome-to-mediator and decision-quality paths separately by legal context, with common relevance-to-likelihood and severity-to-foreseeability paths. Dashed paths have a shared interval containing zero or both context intervals containing zero. The tables report indirect effects and the omitted search-error-preference path, direct outcome paths to likelihood and foreseeability, attitude slopes, and disturbance covariances. Expected evidence is evidence consistent with a self-managed abortion. Unexpected evidence is evidence of a financial crime. Primary sample n per condition: illegal abortion, 68 with no information, 63 with expected evidence, and 63 with unexpected evidence; improper disposal, 71 with no information, 66 with expected evidence, and 67 with unexpected evidence. All 398 participants are in the analysis.
