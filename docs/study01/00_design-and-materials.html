Study 1 Design and Materials

November 18, 2026
Emma Marshall, J.D., M.A.
University of Nebraska-Lincoln

In Study 1, I examined judgments of a search warrant after a pregnancy loss using one affidavit carried forward from Pilot Study 2. I randomly assigned each participant to one of six conditions that crossed search-outcome information with the alleged offense. Table S1D.1 links the design to the study materials, and Figure S1D.1 shows the six conditions.

The Materials and Measures chapter reproduces the stimulus variations, survey questions, and response scales. It includes the registered probability-of-guilt item that participants did not see or answer; the deviations record documents that omission. The materials hub provides document-image previews. The preregistration and registered materials PDF provide the planned design and wording.

Note. None = no outcome information; SMA = self-managed-abortion evidence; Financial = financial-crime evidence. The six condition links below open the corresponding participant-view materials.
