Exploratory Analyses

November 18, 2026
Emma Marshall, J.D., M.A.
University of Nebraska-Lincoln

This chapter reports the exploratory analyses that support the primary Pilot 2 tests. They include the analyses based on causal model theory, the attitude analyses, and the analyses of later judgments.
